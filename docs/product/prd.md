# Product requirements

Hypothesis: Latvian professional apartment managers with 20–100 buildings need a repeatable investigation order for monthly heat use. Buyer: company management. Daily user: portfolio manager/analyst. Technical reviewer: energy specialist. These roles and segment require discovery.

Primary journey: isolated synthetic workspace → ordered buildings → weather reference and quality → assigned review with technical action, user assessment, priority, due date and history → intervention → evidence export. CSV/XLSX imports reach backend validation and commit only after explicit review.

Scope: landing page, deterministic twelve-building/33-month data, period/type/quality/status filters, building detail and accessible chart tables, review tasks/history, interventions, filtered CSV/PDF, audited settings. No sensors or heating-control integration required.

Persistent synthetic disclosure is required on screens and exports. Insufficient history cannot become a reassuring green performance score. Keyboard focus, labels, non-color status text and 1440/1024/390 layouts are required. No fake contact form exists.

Excluded: OCR, billing integration, payment, real-time monitoring, certified energy audit, equipment diagnosis, annual savings proof and actual customer outcome claims. Real-weather import, OIDC browser login, production operations and reviewed Latvian copy are pending.

Pilot success: jointly agreed data usability, review time, actionable-finding rate, false-alarm causes and task completion. Six weeks cannot guarantee annual savings.
