# Meeting Q&A

## 1. Why Latvia?
EN: Latvia is our proposed discovery market. Local manager and expert feedback must validate market fit.

TR hazırlık: Doğrulanmış pazar talebi yok.

## 2. Who is the first customer?
EN: We hypothesize professional managers of 20–100 apartment buildings. A pilot starts with 5–10 authorized buildings.

TR hazırlık: Segment ve kapsamı müşteri sayısıyla karıştırma.

## 3. What problem do you solve?
EN: We aim to reduce the effort of deciding which monthly heat records deserve investigation. We will measure that effort with managers.

TR hazırlık: Zaman kazancı henüz ölçülmedi.

## 4. What data is needed?
EN: Monthly consumption, units, full periods, heated area, source records and documented weather, plus available meter and use changes.

TR hazırlık: Önce gerçek veri envanteri.

## 5. What if only 12 months exist?
EN: The weather model needs 18 seasonally varied training months. Shorter histories get descriptive results or insufficient data.

TR hazırlık: Yeterlilik sınırını saklama.

## 6. Why not Excel?
EN: Excel is a strong alternative. We must test whether validation, explanations and recorded follow-up justify another tool.

TR hazırlık: Üstünlük kanıtlanmış değil.

## 7. How are you different?
EN: The initial scope is monthly file-based heat investigation without required control hardware. Buyers must validate that narrower workflow.

TR hazırlık: Rakibin bilinmeyen özelliğine yok deme.

## 8. Is it an AI product?
EN: The core uses transparent numerical calculations and a constrained weather reference. It works without an AI service.

TR hazırlık: Hesaplanabilir yöntemi anlat.

## 9. How reliable is the model?
EN: Recorded tests establish synthetic and edge-case behavior only. Field performance needs authorized records and specialist review.

TR hazırlık: Saha doğruluğu iddia etme.

## 10. How do you prevent leakage?
EN: Training ends before evaluation begins. A test changes future observations and checks that coefficients remain unchanged.

TR hazırlık: Tarihleri ekranda göster.

## 11. Does a flag mean a fault?
EN: No. Weather, hot water, usage or meter changes may explain consumption. A human reviews before technical action.

TR hazırlık: Teşhis koyma.

## 12. Can six weeks prove savings?
EN: The pilot tests data usability and review usefulness. Annual savings and causal effects need longer monitoring and separate verification.

TR hazırlık: Garanti verme.

## 13. How do you handle privacy?
EN: Pseudonymize building records. Complete agreements, hosting, retention and access configuration before real transfer.

TR hazırlık: GDPR sertifikası yok.

## 14. How is access controlled?
EN: Pilot API verifies external identity and organization membership. Local demo workspaces are isolated. Real IdP setup still needs testing.

TR hazırlık: Browser login henüz yok.

## 15. Do you need sensors?
EN: No new sensor is required for monthly retrospective scope. Better measurements may help later.

TR hazırlık: Donanım işi bu kapsamda yok.

## 16. What is the price?
EN: We will test per-building, portfolio and setup-plus-subscription options. Example prices are interview hypotheses.

TR hazırlık: Maliyet varsayımlarını bil.

## 17. Who pays and uses it?
EN: Company management is the buyer hypothesis; portfolio managers use it, and an energy specialist reviews technical evidence.

TR hazırlık: Alıcı/kullanıcı/uzman ayrımı.

## 18. How will you sell?
EN: Start with discovery conversations and authorized small pilots, potentially through relevant introductions. No signed pilot is claimed.

TR hazırlık: Gönderilmeyen outreach gönderildi deme.

## 19. Who is on the team?
EN: Founder details and confirmed roles still need to be supplied. Local energy and legal-review capacity are open needs.

TR hazırlık: İsim/eğitim/başarı uydurma.

## 20. Is there a Latvian company?
EN: No incorporation information has been supplied. We seek guidance on appropriate legal and operating steps.

TR hazırlık: Şirketleşmeyi doğrula.

## 21. What support is requested?
EN: Relevant manager introductions, a local energy reviewer, and guidance on an appropriate support route and next step.

TR hazırlık: Görüşme kabul/fon değildir.

## 22. What is pilot success?
EN: Agree data usability, review time, expert-rated actionable findings, false-alarm reasons and task-completion targets beforehand.

TR hazırlık: Sayısal hedefleri birlikte kararlaştır.

## 23. What if the data is poor?
EN: Report the issue, narrow scope or stop the model rather than imply reliable performance.

TR hazırlık: Sessiz satır kaybı yok.

## 24. Is it production ready?
EN: The local synthetic demo is reviewable. Real pilot and production each have separate identity, weather, legal and operational gates.

TR hazırlık: Üç durumu ayrı söyle.
