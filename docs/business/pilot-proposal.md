# Portotherm pilot proposal

Provisional brand · Discussion draft · 15 October 2026

## Purpose and scope

Test whether existing monthly heat records support a useful investigation order for a Latvian apartment management company. Proposed scope: 5–10 buildings and six weeks of retrospective review. The 20–100-building customer segment is a hypothesis. No customer or pilot commitment is claimed.

## Data and responsibilities

Manager supplies authorized pseudonymized building IDs, full-month consumption, units, source periods, stable heated area, DHW inclusion and available meter/use/maintenance changes. Prefer 24–36 months, but inventory actual availability first. Shorter history may yield descriptive or insufficient-data results. No resident-level data or new sensor required. Credible weather comparison requires documented source, location/station, coverage and base temperature; demo weather is synthetic.

Founder prepares import, traceable calculations and review/export workflow. Manager verifies source interpretation and assigns a daily user. A local energy specialist reviews plausibility, false alarms and proposed actions. Legal parties, hosting and specialist are still to be agreed. Synthetic demonstration does not establish field performance.

## Six-week plan

| Week | Work | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agree questions, sharing, buildings and inventory | Scope/permissions/data availability |
| 2 | Import, correct quality issues, verify area/weather | Data quality report and dictionary |
| 3 | Build time-separated historical reference | Initial findings, method and coverage |
| 4 | Manager and expert review | Actionability decisions and false-alarm log |
| 5 | Assign reviews, record existing interventions | Task register and monitoring needs |
| 6 | Review value, limits and next steps | Pilot report and commercial go/no-go |

## Measurement and decision

Agree numerical targets before evaluation. Measure usable-data ratio, upload/review time, proportion an expert judges worth action, rejected-flag reasons and task completion. Record denominators and missing data. Do not make six-week success depend on guaranteed energy savings.

Continue if authorized data supports defensible comparison, experts identify useful review work and buyer value justifies support costs. Stop/narrow if quality prevents a baseline, false alarms outweigh value, security/legal gates fail or manager cannot allocate time. Technical validation remains incomplete without real use and specialist feedback.

## Exclusions and commercial scope

Exclude sensors, automatic control, certified audit, OCR, accounting integrations and annual savings verification. Intervention effects may need another heating season and separate measurement/verification design. Fixed pilot/setup plus subscription is a pricing hypothesis. Expert fees, procurement and responsibilities require written agreement.

## Data protection and return

Before transfer agree parties, controller/processor roles, access, hosting, retention, incidents and DPA where applicable. Return normalized data and final reports. Proposed deletion within 30 days after pilot close, including backup treatment, requires agreement and implementation; it is not an existing automated guarantee.

## Immediate request

Identify a willing manager and local energy reviewer, then agree a small authorized sample. Clarify appropriate advisory/support route. No grant, acceptance or Latvian incorporation is assumed.
